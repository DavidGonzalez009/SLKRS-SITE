SLKRS Shootout Qualification Rules

Everything you need to know about qualifying for The SLKRS Shootout.

Qualifying for the Shootout

  • The four (4) teams with the lowest scores after 18 holes will advance to The SLKRS Shootout.
  • Semifinal Matchups:
    • 1st Place vs. 4th Place
    • 2nd Place vs. 3rd Place
  • Winners advance to The SLKRS Shootout Championship.

SLKRS Shootout Tiebreaker

  • If a SLKRS Shootout match ends in a tie, both teams will play one (1) additional playoff hole.
  • If the teams remain tied after the playoff hole, the match will be decided by a Closest to the Pin Tiebreaker.
  • All four (4) players from each team will hit one shot.
  • Only shots that come to rest on the putting green are eligible. If no player from either team comes to rest on the green, both teams will continue hitting one shot per player until at least one ball comes to rest on the green.
  • The team with the single closest eligible shot to the pin will advance.

Eligibility

  • Your team must consist of four (4) players.
  • All four (4) players must play every hole.
  • Teams not meeting these requirements are not eligible.

Tiebreaker Procedure

  1. Lowest score on Hole 18
  2. If still tied, lowest score on Hole 17
  3. If still tied, lowest score on Hole 16
  4. Continue comparing scores in reverse order until the tie is broken.

Official Scorecards

  • Each team is responsible for submitting a completed scorecard immediately after finishing their round.
  • Once submitted, all scorecards are considered final.

Tournament Decisions

  • Any situation not specifically covered by these rules will be decided by SLKRS Golf Tournament Officials.
  • All tournament rulings and decisions are final.
